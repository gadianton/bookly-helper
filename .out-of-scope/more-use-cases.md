# More use cases

The prototype acts only on order status, returns/refunds and password resets; it answers general questions about published store facts (return rules, the banner's shipping cost), and anything else gets a scope reply or a handoff.

## Why
Facts broad, actions deep: the take-home grades depth over breadth, so looking up orders and making returns stays limited to two use cases done well, while stating a fact the store already publishes costs little and keeps live demos from stumbling on common questions.
